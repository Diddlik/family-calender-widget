## Short description (max. 80 characters)

Your family's next events as a widget, filtered by calendars and keywords

## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

Family Calendar Widget shows your family's upcoming events right on your home screen – only the ones you care about.

For each widget, choose the calendars, for example those of every family member, and set keywords. The widget then lists only events whose title contains one of the keywords, in chronological order. Keep track of all medical appointments in the family without scrolling through the whole calendar.

FEATURES
• Multiple widgets, each with its own calendars, keywords and item count
• Preset for medical appointments (German keywords Arzt, Praxis, Impfung) or your own keywords
• Recurring events shown as individual occurrences, all-day events with their date
• Cancelled and declined events are hidden
• Optional reminders per widget, independent of the reminders in your calendar
• Tap an event to open it in your calendar app
• Updates on calendar changes and removes past events
• Resizable, light and dark mode

PRIVACY
• Works with every calendar synced to your device (Google, CalDAV/Nextcloud and more)
• Reads calendars only, never writes
• No internet permission, no ads, no tracking
• Event details stay on your device
